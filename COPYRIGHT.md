## Cultural note
The collection honours India's living folk and tribal art traditions — Warli,
Madhubani/Mithila, Gond, Bhil, Saura, Phad, Pattachitra, Kalamkari, Ajrak,
Phulkari, Kutch embroidery and regional mural/wallpaper idioms. These are
original homages to public cultural traditions; no copyrighted artwork of any
living artist or estate has been copied. 705 tribes are honoured in spirit and
statistics across the site.
